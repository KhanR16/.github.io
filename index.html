About

I am an Assistant Professor of Artificial Intelligence at the National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST), Pakistan. I completed my PhD in Computer Science in 2025, with a thesis titled “Cross-Domain Recommendation using Deep Learning.”

My research focuses on intelligent and personalized systems using deep learning, graph-based modelling and representation learning. My doctoral work addressed data sparsity and cold-start challenges in cross-domain recommender systems by transferring preference information between domains. My broader interests include graph machine learning, generative AI, multimodal AI and trustworthy AI.

I also bring professional experience in data analytics and information systems, including multi-source data integration, analytical dashboards, data quality assessment and decision-support pipelines.

Research & applied projects

Cross-Domain Recommendation with Graph Learning and Contrastive Representation Learning

Doctoral research · 2018–2025

Developed a framework to address sparse target-domain interactions and cold-start users, combining graph-based user–item modelling, behavioural representations, contrastive learning and attention-based feature fusion.

Reported evaluation: Amazon Movies→Books and Movies→Music HR@10 of 0.543 and 0.542, compared with 0.376 and 0.436 for the strongest baseline.

QUROCR — Urdu Nastaliq OCR

Applied VLM / OCR project

Hybrid OCR workflow combining text-region detection, reading-order reconstruction and parameter-efficient fine-tuning of a vision-language model for Urdu Nastaliq, including complex ligatures, diacritics and Arabic-Indic numerals.

Reported results: 23.10% CER and 81.26% WER, compared with zero-shot results of 33.25% CER and 95.22% WER.

CaseFlow — Protection Monitoring & Referral Data Pipeline

2026 · Data engineering and decision support

Project involving KOBO XLSForm collection, Python/SQL ETL, data-quality checks and Power BI reporting for vulnerability screening, severity classification, consent and referral tracking.

Cross-Disciplinary Data Integration Platform

2023 · Data integration

Cloud-oriented ETL and metadata integration across educational, environmental and healthcare sources, using Neo4j graph modelling to support cross-institutional queries.
